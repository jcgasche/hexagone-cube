This study is far from finished. It is one person's first estimate, written to show that the idea deserves a professional study, and several of its figures rest on assumptions that only better data can replace. This chapter lists the work still to do, in order of how much each item could change the result, with the data it needs, why, and who could help. It is kept up to date: each row says where the item stands.

The work still to do, and the data it needs

Each row has a status. Open: nobody is working on it yet. Asked: someone who can help has been contacted. Feedback received: an expert has answered and the study has been changed where needed. The full list of open questions, with the first answer this report gives to each, is in what to verify, point by point.

How to help

  • A pointer to data is the most useful help: a published dataset, a study, a report that answers one of the rows above.
  • A correction: a figure that looks wrong or a rule the study misses. Each one is carried into the model and into both languages.
  • An introduction to someone who works on one of these questions.

Write to hexagone.cube.paris@gmail.com, or open an issue on the study's GitHub page. Those who help are thanked by name in about this study if they agree.
