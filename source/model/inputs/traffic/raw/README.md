## How the two validation aggregates were made

The four quarterly files (Jul 2025 – Jun 2026, about 460,000 to 480,000 rows each) were downloaded in full and summed outside the repository; the raw quarterly files are not kept (about 30 MB each).

- Key: `code_stif_trns`, `code_stif_res`, `code_stif_arret`, `id_zdc` (Q1 2026 names this column `ida`; values like `71370.0` are written `71370`). `libelle_arret` is the first name seen for the key.
- `nb_vald` is summed over all `categorie_titre` values. No non-numeric `nb_vald` values were found.
- The files have no day-type field, so the daily file keeps one row per date and station. In the station file, `lun_ven`, `sam` and `dim` come from the calendar weekday only (public holidays are not removed). `2026S1` is 2026-01-01 to 2026-06-30 (181 days: 129 Mon–Fri, 26 Sat, 26 Sun); `12m` is 2025-07-01 to 2026-06-30 (365 days: 261, 52, 52). The `nb_jours_*` columns give the number of days the station appears in each period.
- A station that changed `id_zdc` between quarters appears twice (for example MAGENTA: 74000 in 2025, 478733 in 2026), and RATP and SNCF validators at the same place are separate rows (for example SAINT-LAZARE `100;110;769` and `800;854;822`).

## Looked for and not found

- RATP "Trafic annuel entrant par station du réseau ferré", recent years: data.ratp.fr now lists only 16 datasets (air quality, toilets, defibrillators and similar), none on traffic. On IDFM, `trafic-annuel-entrant-par-station-annee-2015-sur-le-reseau-ferre-ratp` and `validation-sur-le-reseau-ferre-ratp-trafic-annuel-entrant-par-station-annee-2014` have no records and no attachments. data.gouv.fr has only 2011–2015 versions, with links pointing to those empty datasets or to old data.ratp.fr download links. The IDFM validation files above cover RATP stations (`code_stif_trns` 100) instead.
- TER or Intercités passengers by line or by station pair: nothing on ressources.data.sncf.com (searches "TER", "montées", "comptage", "fréquentation", "intercités"). What exists there: monthly punctuality (`regularite-mensuelle-ter`, `regularite-mensuelle-intercites`), fares (`tarifs-intercites`) and station footfall (`frequentation-gares`, which includes TER and Intercités passengers in each station's total). On data.gouv.fr, the only regional match is "Fréquentation Cars régionaux et TER des Pays de la Loire" (Région des Pays de la Loire, not downloaded).
- TGV passengers by line, axis or city pair: none found. SNCF publishes trains per city pair (`regularite-mensuelle-tgv-aqst`, downloaded), per axis (`regularite-mensuelle-tgv-axes`) and fares (`tarifs-tgv-inoui-ouigo`), but no passenger counts. The Autorité de régulation des transports dataset on data.gouv.fr, "Données du marché français du transport ferroviaire de voyageurs et de marchandises" (id `5afd4b6bc751df5b49337448`, licence fr-lo, resource `https://ftp.autorite-transports.fr/ART_donnees_transport_ferroviaire.xlsx`), could not be downloaded: the ftp.autorite-transports.fr host did not answer.
- Passenger load per section or per train on RER or Transilien ("charge", "taux d'occupation", "affluence"): no dataset on IDFM, SNCF or data.gouv.fr. The SNCF Transilien boardings file (by station and time band) is the closest.
- IDFM history 2015–2025 (`histo-validations-reseau-ferre`, 11 yearly zip files `data-rf-YYYY.zip`) exists but was not downloaded, since the four quarters above already cover a full recent year.
